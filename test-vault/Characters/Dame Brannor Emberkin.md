Brannor Emberkin gave up a mountain hall and a noble title for a suit of plate and a
stretch of dangerous road. She has never once implied that she thinks she got the worse
end of the bargain — and anyone who spends an evening on the pilgrim road under her watch
tends to come around to her way of thinking by morning.

## Early life

Born to the Emberkin, a clan of Mountain Dwarf smiths whose forge-fires had not gone cold
in nine generations, Brannor was expected to inherit the great bellows and a seat on the
hold's council. She was, by every account, extraordinarily good at the work: patient with
the metal, unbothered by the heat, and possessed of a steadiness that the older smiths
called *stonehanded*. What none of them saw coming was the vigil.

At sixteen she sat a night beside a dying pilgrim who had stumbled half-frozen into the
lower gate, and something in his last, unremarkable prayer lodged in her like a splinter of
light. She did not sleep for three days. When she rose, she walked to the reliquary, took
up the old devotional oath that no Emberkin had sworn in living memory, and spoke it aloud
to an empty chapel.

## The oath

The Oath of Devotion is not a gentle promise. It binds the paladin to honesty, courage,
compassion, honor, and duty — not as ideals to aspire toward, but as debts payable on
demand, in full, regardless of circumstance. Brannor treats it exactly that way. She holds
herself to a standard that would break most people, and the only real danger in her
character is that she is sometimes slow to remember that others never swore what she swore.

## The road

She left the hold with her family's blessing, a warhammer she forged herself, and the
Silver Flame reliquary that had once held the dying pilgrim's prayer. For the past four
years she has walked the pilgrim road between the lowland shrines, escorting the faithful,
the desperate, and the occasional scoundrel through country that grows more dangerous every
season. She fights in the front rank, heals with her bare hands, and has buried more
travelers than she will discuss.
